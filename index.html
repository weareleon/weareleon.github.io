weareleon

What is weareleon

A tiny broadcaster that lives on your own computer.

weareleon is a small open-source collective making the kind of software that used to need a control room. TV channels with ad breaks and on-screen graphics. Radio stations with crossfades and jingles. A teletext service. A weather radio that reads the forecast aloud.

Everything runs on hardware you own, from a spare laptop to a cheap VPS, and plays the media already sitting on your drive or in your Jellyfin, Emby or Plex library. No cloud, no subscriptions, no accounts with anybody but yourself.

It started as "stupid stuff ig". It turns out stupid stuff can be pretty useful.

01

Self-hosted, always

Your library, your server, your stream. Nothing phones home.

02

Light enough for an old laptop

Plain Python or Node, ffmpeg and Icecast. No Docker required, nothing heavy to build.

03

Plays nicely with your setup

Speaks M3U, XMLTV, HDHomeRun and Icecast, so Jellyfin, Plex, VLC and TiVimate just work.

04

Feels like real broadcast

Hard starts, bumpers, tickers, "Up next" cards, station idents. The good bits of TV and radio.

CH 01 · TV · FLAGSHIPTypeScript

tvmanager

Turn your Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or local media into real live TV channels. Build channels in a web dashboard and watch them in Plex (as an HDHomeRun tuner), Jellyfin, TiVimate, VLC or any IPTV player.

  • Weekly blocks, one-off specials and hard starts on the minute
  • Commercial breaks with bumpers, promos and "Up next" cards
  • Live-drawn banners, countdowns, tickers, clocks and watermarks
  • Gapless playback, always on air with ~48h of schedule

A lightweight, self-hosted alternative to AzuraCast. Multi-station internet radio without the bloat, and without needing Docker.

  • BPM-aware crossfading and shuffle
  • Playlists, day-and-time scheduling and jingles

FM 03 · RADIOPython

SVAR

Skånes Väder- och Alarmradio. A NOAA Weather Radio-style station for southern Sweden that reads live SMHI forecasts and warnings aloud, 24/7.

  • Spoken Swedish via Piper TTS
  • Vintage PA/EAS filter chain with static bed
  • No API keys: SMHI open data is free

FM 04 · RADIOPython

last.fm/radio

A personal radio station built from your Last.fm scrobbles. Pulls your top tracks, streams them shuffled and gapless to Icecast, and refreshes every 30 minutes.

  • Dashboard with now playing and recent scrobbles
  • Continuous ffmpeg stream, no gaps

Heads up: from 1 December 2026, Last.fm API keys need a Last.fm Pro subscription on the account that owns the key. Last.fm is independent and the free API was getting too costly to run. Read their announcement.

How it fits together

From a folder of files to a channel on your TV.

Every weareleon project follows the same simple chain, so they work alongside each other and the tools you already run.

01 · SOURCE

Your media

A music folder, a video drive, or a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex library. Or live data, like SMHI weather.

02 · SCHEDULE

weareleon

tvmanager, leonCAST and friends decide what airs when, add breaks, idents and graphics.

03 · ENCODE

ffmpeg

Splices everything into one continuous stream with crossfades and overlays drawn live.

04 · TUNE IN

Anywhere

Icecast for radio, M3U + XMLTV or HDHomeRun for TV. Watch in Jellyfin, Plex, VLC or a browser.
